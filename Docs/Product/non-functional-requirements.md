Functional requirements (features and user stories) are in [`features.md`](features.md). This page lists how well Farol must do them: each requirement has a measurable target and the test, demo or metric that proves it. Categories follow the ISO/IEC 25010 quality characteristics; observability (assignment §6.7) and privacy (RGPD) are listed on their own because the assignment asks for them separately. The architecture drivers behind most of them are in [`architecture.md` §1](../Technical/architecture.md#1-architecture-drivers).

## 4. Decided on 24 October 2026 (Tiago)

- **Success criteria confirmed:** SC1, SC2, SC4, SC6 and SC11 ([`product-vision.md` §5](product-vision.md#5-measurable-success-criteria)) and the report rate limit of US-48 (5 per 10 min per account). Only SC3 (30 s) had been set by the team before.
- **SC6 raised from 10 s to 15 s** (NFR-04): participant pages keep polling every 10 s, so the worst case (one interval plus the server time) fits, and the J3 peak of about 1,300 req/s does not change.
- **Outbox alert:** oldest pending row above **60 s for 2 min** (NFR-34), joining the 60 s of PR #52 and the 2 min window of the observability research, so a short peak does not alert.
- **Accessibility (NFR-25):** WCAG 2.1 AA on the main pages (*Ask for help*, the venue map and the backoffice list of help requests), owner Tiago.
- **Availability and recovery (NFR-12, NFR-13)** stay, measured from local runs and a recovery drill, because delivery is local only ([ADR-011](../Technical/adr/0011-deployment-and-infrastructure.md)); no cloud high availability is claimed.
- **Weak-network mode (F11, SC10)** stays out of the list while it is a Could; it is added only if F11 is built.

## 5. Validated with the team on 26 October 2026

Tiago went through the proposed targets with Tomás, Guerra and Bruno, the owners of their sources. All confirmed:

- **NFR-03:** ≥ 95 % valid suggestions stored within 30 s over a one-hour window, as defined in ADR-005 (#145). **Abstentions count as misses**, including an abstention on a help request with no text, so the target asks the model to suggest whenever it can.
- **NFR-06:** saving a help request, p95 ≤ 2 s over 15 min. **NFR-12:** ≥ 99.5 % successful creates over a rolling 7 days, in service hours.
- **NFR-11:** the AI timeout, retry and circuit-breaker values of `architecture.md` §9.1 (connect 2 s, response 10 s, 2 attempts), whatever model K1 picks.
- **NFR-13:** code rollback ≤ 10 min, stateful restore ≤ 60 min, no committed rows lost on a restart.
- **NFR-19:** the help-request consent when joining an event, an account with an e-mail and a display name only, and the retention periods of the assessment §7 (help-request content 30 days after the event closes, logs 14 days, traces 7 days, Keycloak login events 30 days).
- **NFR-31:** WireMock as the simulator of the Porto Digital API and the AI provider (guide J4).
- **NFR-33:** Micrometer/OpenTelemetry with Prometheus/Grafana, run locally (ADR-012).

Then, the same day, the items that had no value yet:

- **NFR-13, backup:** the proposal of PR #130 (`local-delivery.md` §6): a dump of the six databases before every demo and every update, queued messages not covered; on disk loss the last dump is recovered, with no RPO guaranteed for what came after it; dumps kept at most 30 days, so account deletions reach them (assessment §7).
- **NFR-30:** the procedure and smoke test of PR #130, **with no time limit**.
- **NFR-34:** the six other alerts proposed in the observability research, with their triggers; only on the Grafana of the demo machine, no external notifications. The spend alert is dropped: delivery is local.
- **NFR-20:** **local Ollama for now** (K1), so the hosted-provider review is not needed. Tiago tells the team if this changes.

Then, still on 26 October 2026, Tiago aligned the list with Tomás's plans, merged that day (#148, #149), which are now the reference:

- **NFR-19, telemetry retention:** traces 24 h instead of 7 days; container logs rotated at 10 MiB × 2 files, and sanitised log exports deleted within 14 days, instead of 14 days of application logs; metrics 7 days or 2 GiB. The help-request, account and Keycloak periods do not change.
- **NFR-33:** the stack of the observability plan, with Tempo for traces, the OpenTelemetry Collector and Alertmanager, and no Loki.
- **NFR-34:** the alert rules of the observability plan §4 replace the six alerts of the research: the create-failure alert (> 5 % over 5 min) is dropped; useful triage below 95 %, urgent suggestion failure, missing telemetry and stale transport data are added; the DLQ and broker triggers follow the plan.
- **NFR-20:** local Ollama stays for now; the final provider and model are chosen through the evaluation plan (#149).
