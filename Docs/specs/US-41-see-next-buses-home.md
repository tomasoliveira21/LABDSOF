## 1. Source

**Story:** as a **participant** (P1 · Rita), I want to see the city buses on the routes that serve the venue, so that I don't walk home or drive.

**This PR:** an EventAdmin, or a member with `Transport.Edit`, adds the event's bus routes through the API; any member of the event sees, on the event's page of the web app, the buses on those routes within 2 km of the venue, on a street map and in a list, each with how far it is from the venue and the time of its last position. The source gives live positions, not timetables (O6), so nothing here predicts an arrival.

## 6. Out of scope

- Stops of a route and shuttles (US-20, US-37); changing or removing a route (US-37); a team form for routes (proposed on #98).
- The stale warning and "Data from HH:MM" on the page (US-42); the source-down warning for the team (US-43).
- The participant's own location, the direction of a bus, predicted arrivals: the source gives positions only (O6).
- Refusing a disabled account at once: `UserStatusChanged` is still only logged by Transports (#193 D3); the token's expiry applies until its story.
- The CSP header on the web container (assessment action 11); this story only adds what the policy must allow (D13).
