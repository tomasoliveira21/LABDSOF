# The seven report type codes, and two team-write decisions

**Recorded:** 21 November 2026, with decision 3 added on 22 November 2026 · **Source:** the open points of [spec US-17](../specs/US-17-team-permissions.md) (D3, D5) and [spec US-16](../specs/US-16-teams.md) (D2, D3), raised on PR [#243](https://github.com/tomasoliveira21/LABDSOF/pull/243) and PR [#242](https://github.com/tomasoliveira21/LABDSOF/pull/242); the report type list was agreed by the owner of the Reports stories, who consumes it, in the review of #243. This is a record of decisions, not minutes of a meeting.

Decision 1 is here because it turns a single service's proposal into a **published contract that another service must follow**, which no spec or changelog entry is the right place to settle.

## Decisions

| # | Question | Options | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The seven report type codes had been Events' proposal since [spec #192](../specs/192-events-service.md) D6 and appeared in no product document: [`features.md` §6.1](../Product/features.md#61-what-can-be-reported) listed report types by **label only**. They are in `openapi.yaml` (`ReportType`) and in `team.changed.schema.json`, so Reports (US-47 onwards) has to use the same strings | (a) confirm the seven codes as the contract for every service; (b) keep them as Events' proposal until the first report story (US-47) needs them; (c) choose a different naming scheme first | **(a)**. The codes are `QueueAtEntrance`, `CrowdedArea`, `WcProblem`, `NoWater`, `Hazard`, `TransportProblem`, `WrongInformation`, one per row of `features.md` §6.1, and they are now written into it. Agreed by the owner of the Reports stories, who consumes them through `TeamChanged` and serves them in US-47 onwards. (b) was rejected because a team's `Reports.Review` permission already stores and publishes these strings today, so they are a contract in fact; leaving them marked *proposed* in two schemas while the contract header called them confirmed only told a consumer two different things. Expiry per type (§6.1) stays open and is not part of this decision |
| 2 | May an event that is `Suspended` or `Closed` still have its teams and their permissions changed? No product document covers it; it was read from US-09 (*an abandoned or misused event cannot be used*) and US-10 (edits on *a draft or published event*) | (a) refuse every team write with `409`; (b) allow writes in any status; (c) refuse only on `Closed` | **(a)**, as [US-16](../specs/US-16-teams.md) D2 had proposed and applied. Reading the teams is unaffected, and an EventAdmin still sees the list in any status. (b) would let an abandoned or misused event keep being reorganised and a closed event's history keep moving; (c) leaves the suspended case, which is the one US-09 names |
| 3 | Two requests that change the same team at once (a rename racing a delete, or two permission writes) each read the team, bumped the version and wrote it. Both committed, so **two `TeamChanged` carried one version with different states**; a consumer applies a copy only when its version is strictly newer, so it kept the first and dropped the second for good. A lost `DELETE` left a deleted team active in Reports' copy for ever, since a deleted team never changes again | (a) every write matches the version it read, and a write that matches no row is refused; (b) lock the row for update while the use case runs; (c) let the consumer reconcile | **(a)**. Every team write carries `AND version = :version - 1`; zero rows changed answers `409` when the team is still live at another version and `404` when it was deleted in between, and neither publishes anything. This keeps the rule every consumer already assumes (API outline §7.3: one version is one state) without holding a lock across a use case, which (b) would. (c) was rejected because the consumers cannot reconcile what they never receive. Found in the reviews of #242 and #243 on 22 November 2026; it corrects US-16 D3, which had said this race answered `404` |

## Applied

- Decision 1: [`features.md` §6.1](../Product/features.md#61-what-can-be-reported) (the codes beside the labels); `backend/events/contracts/openapi.yaml` (`ReportType` and the header); `backend/events/contracts/events/team.changed.schema.json`; [spec US-17](../specs/US-17-team-permissions.md) D6; `CHANGELOG.md` (#66).
- Decision 2: [spec US-16](../specs/US-16-teams.md) D2; `backend/events/contracts/openapi.yaml` (the `409` on `DELETE …/teams/{teamId}` and on the permissions `PUT`, and the `Conflict` response); `CHANGELOG.md` (#65, #66).
- Decision 3: `JdbcTeams.replace`, `JdbcTeams.delete` and `JdbcTeams.replacePermissions`; `Teams.Write.CONFLICT` and `TeamChangedConcurrentlyException` (409); [spec US-16](../specs/US-16-teams.md) D3 and its edge-case matrix; [spec US-17](../specs/US-17-team-permissions.md) B1 note and matrix; the `Conflict` response of `openapi.yaml`; `CHANGELOG.md` (#65, #66).

## Still open

- **Which team is the default when two teams handle the same help category** (US-17 D5): several teams may handle one category and several may review one report type, which this work does not refuse. The tie-break stays an open product decision, for US-29 (#76).
- **Expiry per report type** (`features.md` §6.1), untouched by decision 1.
- The entity states of 15 November 2026 §4 are still a proposal ([#198](https://github.com/tomasoliveira21/LABDSOF/issues/198)).
