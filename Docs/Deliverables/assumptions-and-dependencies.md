## 1. Product assumptions

From the [Problem and Opportunity Report §5](problem-opportunity-report-queima.md#critical-assumptions). The most critical are **A2, A3 and A4**: if any of them fails, the product loses its reason to exist. Validation status from [`product-vision.md` §8](../Product/product-vision.md#8-research-status).

| # | Assumption | Risk | Planned validation | Status (31 October 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Organisations accept configuring and maintaining their event on the platform (map, transport, team) | High | Interview and conversation with organisations | Not validated: no conversation with an organisation is recorded |
| A2 | Participants check the application during the night, even in adverse conditions (alcohol, battery, noise) | High | Survey and prototype | Supported as an intention: 87 % would use the app during the night (survey Q7, n = 31); the barriers are battery, installing another app and weak network (report, *User Research*) |
| A3 | Participants adopt one application shared by several events, opened through a link or QR code | High | Survey | Partly supported: 39 % name installing another app as a barrier, which supports opening by link or QR; one app for several events was not asked directly (report, *User Research*) |
| A4 | Help requests made through the application reach the right team faster than through the current channels | High | Volunteer interview and simulation | Not validated: the first reaction to a problem is people nearby, not a phone (survey Q5); the interview with a security volunteer is still to do (product vision §8) |
| A5 | Each organisation has a usable venue map (image or PDF) | Medium | No method planned yet (the report rates it easy to validate) | Not validated. The design takes an image or a one-page PDF ([ADR-007](../Technical/adr/0007-venue-map-representation.md), proposed) |
| A6 | Real-time information (transport, queues) can be entered or estimated by the event team, without direct integration with the operators | Medium | No method planned yet (the report rates it medium) | Changed for buses: live positions come from the Porto Digital API ([ADR-008](../Technical/adr/0008-transport-data-source.md)); event shuttles are still entered by the event team (ADR-008, decision 3) |
| A7 | AI features are more useful than a non-AI alternative and do not mislead users | Medium | Simulated dataset, compared with a non-AI alternative | Dataset of 100 synthetic cases and a reproducible baseline in the [triage evaluation](../Research/architecture-investigation/evaluation/README.md); label review and model runs pending (guide K2, [ADR-005](../Technical/adr/0005-ai-triage-provider-and-evaluation.md)) |
| A8 | The mobile network holds up at peak, or the application works in degraded or offline mode | Medium | Tests and reports | Not validated. Designed for: data shown with its update time (SC7), light polling at the peak ([`architecture.md` §10.1](../Technical/architecture.md#101-peak-load-and-scaling-j3)); offline mode is F11, a *Could* (SC10) |
| A9 | The same product serves different events through configuration alone, without custom development | Medium | No method planned yet (the report rates it medium) | Not validated. Designed for: per-event data, roles and teams ([`features.md` §4](../Product/features.md#4-roles-teams-and-permissions-22-october-2026), [ADR-003](../Technical/adr/0003-database-and-data-ownership.md)) |

Other assumptions, kept in their own documents: the cost assumptions ([`operating-cost-estimation.md` §2](operating-cost-estimation.md#2-assumptions)) and the peak-load assumptions ([`architecture.md` §10.1](../Technical/architecture.md#101-peak-load-and-scaling-j3)).
